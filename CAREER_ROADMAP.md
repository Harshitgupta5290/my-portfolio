# Career Roadmap — Harshit Gupta
> Updated: May 2026 | Target Role: AI Engineer + Full Stack Developer | Goal: Top 1% globally — $200K–$350K+

---

## What This Roadmap Is For

**AI Engineer** = You build AI-powered products. RAG pipelines, LLM integrations, agents, fine-tuning, model deployment, AI APIs.
**Full Stack Developer** = You build the full product — React/Next.js frontend, Python/Node backend, databases, and deploy it yourself.

This is not a DevOps roadmap. You don't need Kubernetes certs. You don't need Snowflake or dbt. Those are separate career tracks. Every cert and project here directly serves the AI Engineer + Full Stack path.

---

## The Honest Truth About Top 1%

- 60% — Shipped AI products with real users (visible, live, working)
- 20% — Platform mastery validated by hard certs
- 15% — System design and problem-solving depth
- 5%  — Communication and documentation

**The trap**: Collecting certs without building things = red flag to every senior hiring manager.
**The play**: Stack the hardest AI certs while simultaneously shipping full-stack AI products people actually use.

## What Not To Do

- Don't pursue Kubernetes (CKA/CKS) — that's an SRE/DevOps track, not AI Engineer or Full Stack.
- Don't pursue Snowflake, dbt, or data engineering certs — that's a Data Engineer track.
- Don't skip the hard exams — TensorFlow cert is a coding exam, not multiple choice. These are the ones that differentiate you.
- Don't collect only AWS Practitioner-tier certs — they don't unlock the salary jumps.
- Don't front-load certifications before shipping products — a cert with no project behind it raises questions, not offers.
- Don't let certs expire — most renew every 2–3 years. Set reminders.
